# Internship Candidates — Summary for Management

Three first- and second-year university students took a 90-minute supervised test. It covered general knowledge, short written explanations, finding mistakes in code, and three small programming tasks. They then submitted their answers for review and were allowed to improve them based on the reviewer's feedback, with internet help permitted.

We looked at two things:
- **Test score:** how they did on their own, under test conditions.
- **Working habits:** how well they followed instructions, responded to feedback and organised their work.

## At a glance

| Candidate | Score on their own | Score after feedback | Working habits | Overall |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Alex** | **79%** | 86% | Needs improvement | Strongest technical skills |
| **Angad** | 61% | 75% | Excellent | Best attitude and habits |
| **Dyllon** | 57% | 83% | Needs improvement | Improved the most; needs a follow-up check |

## Alex
- **Strengths:** Solved all the practical tasks correctly on the first try. The most reliable problem-solver of the group.
- **Weaknesses:** Explanations were brief and sometimes surface-level. Did not always follow instructions on where and how to submit work, and needed repeated reminders.
- **In short:** The most capable today; needs coaching on following team processes.

## Angad
- **Strengths:** Gave the most thorough explanations. Was honest about what he didn't know instead of guessing. Followed instructions exactly, fixed all feedback in one go, and was used by the reviewer as the example for the others.
- **Weaknesses:** Made more technical mistakes than Alex, including two tasks that did not fully work.
- **In short:** The best habits and attitude; technical skills will grow with mentoring.

## Dyllon
- **Strengths:** Submitted first. Responded to feedback quickly and politely, and learned from feedback given to other candidates.
- **Weaknesses:** On his own he scored lowest and said he was unfamiliar with several basic topics. Most of his improvement came after internet help was allowed. Needed several rounds of feedback to get his submission right, though this is typical for someone new to the tools.
- **To check:** Part of his original work closely matches a well-known answer that is published online, which doesn't fit with the basic topics he said he didn't know. This is not proof of anything. A short live exercise in the interview would settle it.
- **In short:** Eager and responsive; confirm his skill level before deciding.

## Recommendation
1. **Alex:** recommend. Strongest skills; will need guidance on team processes.
2. **Angad:** recommend if we can provide mentoring. His habits make him easy to develop.
3. **Dyllon:** decide after a short live exercise in the interview.
